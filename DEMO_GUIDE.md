# MedBridge — 2-minute Demo Guide

Keep the demo under two minutes and show the product rather than reading slides.

## 0:00–0:15 — Problem
"MedBridge helps people manage recurring medicines and request refills from nearby verified pharmacies."

## 0:15–0:45 — Consumer flow
- Sign in as the demo patient.
- Open Medicines.
- Add/select a medicine.
- Tap Refill.
- Find a nearby pharmacy.
- Submit the refill request.

## 0:45–1:05 — Pharmacy flow
- Open the pharmacy account.
- Show the incoming refill request.
- Accept/reserve inventory.
- Show the reservation state.

## 1:05–1:25 — Pickup/history
- Return to the patient.
- Show the reservation.
- Mark pickup from the pharmacy side.
- Show refill history / updated medicine stock.

## 1:25–1:50 — RevenueCat
- Open Premium.
- Show the RevenueCat paywall with monthly and annual packages.
- Complete a test purchase using the configured RevenueCat test environment.
- Show `consumer_premium` entitlement active.
- Open one or two Premium features such as family/caregiver, advanced reminders, or Travel Mode.

## 1:50–2:00 — Close
Show the clean home screen and say:
"MedBridge keeps medicine refills simple while RevenueCat powers a focused Premium subscription experience."
